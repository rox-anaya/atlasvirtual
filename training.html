Becoming a Pilot

Training Program

Our structured two-stage certification ensures every Atlas Virtual pilot meets our professional standards before operations. All new pilots must complete our evaluation process after application review.

01

Stage 1: Theory Exam

Knowledge Test Pass Mark Required

Complete our theoretical knowledge exam covering aviation basics, Infinite Flight procedures, and Atlas Virtual standard operating procedures (SOPs).

02

Stage 2: Check-Ride

Practical Test Supervised

Demonstrate your flying skills in a supervised check-ride conducted by one of our certified examiners, evaluated on flight planning, procedures, and aircraft handling.

Atlas Virtual Airline Family Certification

Upon passing both stages, you officially join the Atlas Virtual family. Receive your rank assignment, Pilot Handbook, and full access to all VA global operations.

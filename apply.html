Pilot Requirements

01 Minimum age — 13 years old (per IFC rules)
02 Trust Level — Active member of the Infinite Flight Community (IFC)
03 Flight Grade — Grade 2 or above in Infinite Flight
04 Subscription — Must own a legal copy of Infinite Flight Pro
05 Activity — Minimum 1 flight per month to stay active in the VA
06 Must NOT be on the IFVARB Blacklist or Watchlist

What Happens Next

Application reviewed by our team via IFC
If accepted, receive Cadet trainee rank
Complete Stage 1 theoretical exam
Complete Stage 2 check-ride flight
Receive full certification and operational rank

Important Note

Discord access and the Pilot Handbook are provided after you complete training. All application communication is through IFC only. You must not be on the IFVARB Blacklist or Watchlist to apply.
